## Rules (so it lands, not spam)
1. One real, personalized first line per company (a product, project, or their accreditation work). Never send the same line twice.
2. Email a **person** where possible — find the Director of Regulatory Affairs / Quality Manager / Lab Manager / founder (small firms) / Talent Acquisition on LinkedIn. Otherwise `careers@` addressed to "Hiring Manager, [Regulatory/Quality]".
3. Lead with your proof, not your need. Keep it under ~150 words. One clear, low-friction ask.
4. Follow up **once**, ~5–7 business days later, two lines.
5. Email + LinkedIn only — never put your phone number in outreach.
6. Quality over quantity: 12–15 personalized beats 100 generic.
